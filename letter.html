A Letter for Krishu

A little something, written just for you ✨

Hey there, I know aaj tera bday nhi hai, bcz tu apne birthday Wale din ghar pe nhi rahegi. But fir bhi me tere ko gift dunga, just so you know I care. Bohot si chize hai likhne ko, but kya likhu samajh nhi aa raha. I know hamne bohot Kam time spend Kiya hai ek dusre ke sath but jo bhi kiya hai vo quality time tha. Bhai mujhe aaj bhi yakeen nhi hota ki koi mujhe dhundte hue aaya, like just so you, tu aai mujhe msg kiya aur bola " Bhai yrr me tujhe kitne din se dhund rahi thi ". I was soo surprised ki mere jaise insaan ko bhi koi yaad karta hai, it genuinely made me happy. And haa ek chiz bata du tumhe ki mene tera birth date calendar pe save Kiya tha isi wajah se tere ko 12 baje b'day wish Kiya, mujhe yaad tha okay. And then conversation kept going and going, itna time kab nikal gaya pata nhi chala. Hamne boards sath me diye, padhai sath me ki Video calls pe but maza aaya. Hamne masti ki, it was like a happy era of my life. And and and tune jab mere birthday pe mere liye letter likha tha, ohh goshhh tujhe nhi pata bhai me kitna khush tha us time, tu mera chehra dekhti na me kitna blush kr raha tha. And then...tere hostel jaane ka time aa gaya, tune mujhe bataya, me dukhi hua but fir realise hua ki tu apne future ke liye jaa rahi hai, a better version of you and I definitely wanted to see that. Mene khud ko samjhaya and accept Kiya, but fir bhi teri chinta roz lagi rehti hai mujhe. Tu kese rehti hogi, achhe se khati hogi ki nahi, achhe se sleep leti hogi ki nhi, everything made me worried. It's not like ki me jyada dukhi tha, I was happy to see you improving. Fir mujhe hamari memories yaad aati hai, jab ham dono ne sath me first time call pe baat ki, dude it was a 1 and half hour call, pehli baar kisi dost se itni der baat ki thi, the laugh, the jokes, the emotions, bohot yaad aati h yrr. But fir tune mujhe console kiya, 2 years ka wait until we both will pass 12th. I agreed bcz I know we'll make it. Ham firse wese baate karenge, ham firse sath me masti karenge, I felt happy to hear that. And now.. we are here, tu aati h chutti me, ham baate karte hai, tu fir chali jaati hai. But dosti nhi tuti abhi tak, that's something special. Ohh well ye letter to happy birthday wala tha, ye sab likhna zaruri nhi tha, but likhne ka mn tha to likh diya. Happy birthday Krishu, happy birthday. -Bobo Bear.
